    <h2>Youth night every Friday</h2>
    <p>Next Generation is our youth ministry, where we gather every Friday for an evening full of fellowship, activities, conversation and snacks.</p>
    <p>We believe in the next generation and want to create a place where young people can grow, both as people and in their faith.</p>

    <h2>What do we do together?</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Hanging out and talking</strong> – chilling on the sofas, games, fika.</li>
        <li><strong>Activities</strong> – from table tennis tournaments to movie nights and outings.</li>
        <li><strong>Conversations about life and faith</strong> – at a level where everyone can join in, whatever your background.</li>
        <li><strong>Food or snacks</strong> – no one goes home hungry.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Who is it for?</h2>
    <p>Next Generation is for you if you're around secondary or high school age (roughly 13–19). You don't have to be a Christian or have been to church before – come just as you are. And feel free to bring a friend.</p>

    <aside class="contact-card">
        <h3>When and where?</h3>
        <p><strong>Fridays at 19:00</strong> – every week unless otherwise noted.<br>Elimkyrkan, Klockvägen 1, Mantorp.</p>
        <p>Special evenings and outings show up in the <a href="/kalender">calendar</a>.</p>
    </aside>

    <aside class="contact-card">
        <h3>First time?</h3>
        <p>It's perfectly fine to come without knowing anyone – the leaders will meet you at the door and introduce you to the gang. It costs nothing, and you don't have to stay the whole evening.</p>
    </aside>

    <p style="text-align:center; font-size:18px; margin-top:24px;"><strong>See you on Friday!</strong></p>
