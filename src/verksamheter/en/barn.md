    <h2>Children's ministry at Elimkyrkan</h2>
    <p>Hoppet is our Sunday school for children during the service. Here the children get a time of their own, with fellowship, play, joy and activities suited just for them.</p>
    <p>The service begins at 16:00, and every other Sunday, on even weeks, the children head up to our wonderful upper floor. A fun and safe environment full of energy and joy awaits them there.</p>
    <p>Children aged 0–3 need to have an adult with them, but all ages are warmly welcome.</p>
    <p>A warm welcome to Hoppet!</p>

    <h2>What happens at Hoppet?</h2>
    <p>We want every child to feel seen, safe and included from the very first moment. On a typical Sunday we mix:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Bible stories</strong> – told in a way that suits children, often with pictures, songs or movement.</li>
        <li><strong>Crafts and creativity</strong> – something the children can take home with them.</li>
        <li><strong>Play</strong> – both free play and guided activities.</li>
        <li><strong>Fika</strong> – a little break with fruit or juice.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Who is it for?</h2>
    <p>Hoppet is for children up to and including early primary school age. When the group is large, we often split up by age so the activities fit everyone. Parents and siblings are always welcome to join in, especially with the youngest ones.</p>

    <aside class="contact-card">
        <h3>When and where?</h3>
        <p><strong>Sundays at 16:00</strong> – every other week, on even weeks.<br>Elimkyrkan, Klockvägen 1, Mantorp.</p>
        <p>Have a look at the <a href="/kalender">calendar</a> to see the next session.</p>
    </aside>

    <aside class="contact-card">
        <h3>First time?</h3>
        <p>No registration needed – come when you can, and it costs nothing. Feel free to mention at the entrance that it's your first time, and we'll help you find your way.</p>
