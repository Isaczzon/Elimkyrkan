    <p>Our second-hand shop is called <strong>Axplocket</strong> and is located in the red building right next to Mantorp train station.</p>
    <p><strong>Magasinvägen 3, 595 57 Mantorp</strong></p>

    <h2>Opening hours</h2>
    <ul class="hours-list">
        <li><strong>Wednesdays</strong> 13:00 – 16:00</li>
        <li><strong>Saturdays</strong> 10:00 – 13:00</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Open more or less all year round.</p>

    <h2>You'll find a bit of everything here</h2>
    <p>Glassware, china, furniture, toys, lamps, tools, books – just about anything you can imagine! Of course we also have a ”guys' corner” with everything from golf clubs to tech gadgets.</p>

    <!-- ELIM-BLOCKS -->

    <h2>Our purpose – charity</h2>
    <p>Axplocket is run entirely by volunteers, and our purpose is to raise money for charity and send aid shipments. For example:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Child Safe</strong> – working in Thailand and Laos. They stand on the child's side and work to prevent trafficking.</li>
        <li><strong>Village of Hope</strong> – Estonia. Drug rehabilitation on a Christian foundation.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>That is why we are grateful for everything that is donated. What you hand in helps someone in need. Your gift can help a child who might otherwise become a victim of sex trafficking, or an adult whose life has taken a wrong turn.</p>

    <h2>Clothing collection</h2>
    <p>Every year we have the joy of collecting clothes, shoes and more, which we have then driven to places such as Estonia and Ukraine. Times and circumstances change, so what we can say for certain is that everything collected will go to charity in some way – though not always to Estonia or Ukraine specifically.</p>
    <p>Would you like to help us help? If you have clothes, shoes, bedding and the like, you are welcome to drop them off at our shop by Mantorp commuter station.</p>
    <p><strong>Please note:</strong> we only accept items that are intact, clean and in working condition. We do not accept clothes from October through April.</p>
    <p>When needed, we also arrange special collection days. You'll find information about these on the shop's website, the Facebook group ”Loppis - Axplocket”, ads in the local press and posters around town.</p>
    <p><em>Thank you for helping us help!</em></p>

    <h2>Donating items</h2>
    <p>If you have things you would like to give away, you are very welcome to bring them during regular opening hours. You can also get in touch if you need help transporting them to us.</p>
